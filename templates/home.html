The problem

Students watch hours of lectures and still fail the exam.

Watching feels like understanding

While the teacher explains, everything makes sense. But recognising an idea is not the same as being able to explain it — and without practice most of it is forgotten within days.

Long videos waste revision time

Finding the 4 minutes where one topic was explained means scrubbing through a 60–90 minute recording again, the night before the exam.

AI chatbots just give the answer

Ask ChatGPT and you get the full answer instantly. It feels helpful, but you skip the thinking that actually makes knowledge stick.

Our solution

From passive video to proven understanding in 5 steps.

STEP 1

Import a lecture

Paste a YouTube link or upload an MP4. Speech is transcribed automatically.

STEP 2

STEP 5
