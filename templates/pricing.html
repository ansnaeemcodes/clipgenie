Frequently Asked Questions

Why won't ClipGenie just give me the answer like ChatGPT?

Because ChatGPT does the thinking for you. Studies in cognitive neuroscience show that when a student is handed an answer, retention drops by over 80% within 48 hours. ClipGenie forces you to retrieve the concept yourself, locking it permanently into long-term memory.

Can I import my own university lecture recordings?

Yes! You can paste any public video link (YouTube, Facebook, Vimeo) or upload your recording directly as an MP4, WebM, or MOV file. ClipGenie automatically extracts the concepts and builds your practice studio in seconds.

What if a concept is discussed in multiple parts of the video?

ClipGenie’s curriculum intelligence detects when a concept is revisited later in the talk with new examples or synthesis, linking all related video scenes under that single concept card.
